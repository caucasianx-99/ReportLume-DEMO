About these numbers

Figures are cumulative observations of included individual X posts at the time of export, not account-wide monthly traffic. Posts are grouped by publication month, not by the month in which their impressions occurred. They do not demonstrate causation.

Missing measurements are shown as —, not zero. A recorded zero remains 0. Authorship, post/reply labels and content categories are entered by the agency; other deliverables are self-reported. Targets are reference points set by the agency, not verified contractual results.
